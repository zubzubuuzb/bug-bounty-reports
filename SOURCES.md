# Sources

## Primary disclosed-report corpus

- Archive used to locate and copy publicly disclosed HackerOne report pages:
  https://github.com/ajaysenr/HackerOne-Disclosed-Reports
- Canonical originals: https://hackerone.com/reports/{id}
- Catalog coverage in this snapshot: 587 reports disclosed in 2025 + 672 in 2026 (1,259 total metadata records).
- Full markdown bodies included for a high-signal subset of 79 reports (highest bounty / severity / votes).

These HackerOne pages were made public through HackerOne coordinated disclosure. Keep attribution and original URLs.

## Writeup summaries

Medium / news / gist writeups are **summarized**, not copied in full, because they are typically copyrighted. Use the URL field to fetch the original if your training license allows it.
